WHO WE ARE

Trust should accelerate transformation—not slow it down.

Sun Spectre is a focused advisory company working across AI governance, cybersecurity, vCISO leadership, IT audit, GRC and digital risk.

Traditional consulting often separates strategy, security, audit and compliance into different conversations. Modern enterprises cannot afford that fragmentation. We connect executive decisions with technical reality so leaders can understand what matters, strengthen the control environment and move forward with confidence.

Our model is deliberately senior, practical and evidence-led: smaller teams, direct access to experienced practitioners and outputs designed to change decisions rather than simply fill a slide deck.
